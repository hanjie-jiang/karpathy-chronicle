Method and limits

Sources. His X posts, talks, interviews and writing. X quotes are checked word for word against an archive of his posts before each build. Talk quotes come from third-party or machine transcripts and are marked as such.

Dates. Post dates are in Beijing time (UTC+8), so a post can appear one day after its US date.

Not exhaustive. Replies, image-only posts and most long recordings have not been read. Leaving something out does not mean he never said it. Public statements show what he expressed and when, not when he privately learned it.
